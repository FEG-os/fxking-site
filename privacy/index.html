FXKING Publisher – Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

FXKING Publisher is a private tool used only by the FxKing channel owner. It uploads and schedules FxKing's own videos to FxKing's own YouTube channel.

  • Data used: permission to upload videos to, and read basic details of, the owner's YouTube channel (Google OAuth scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly).
  • Storage: the access key is stored only on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared, or sent to anyone else.
  • Other users: none. The tool collects nothing from viewers or other users.
  • Removing access: revoke anytime at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Contact: fxkingflores@gmail.com

FXKING Publisher's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
